# Code of Conduct

This project's expectations are those of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/); that
document is the authoritative text and applies in full. What follows is how it operates here, not a
replacement for it.

## Expected

- Critique the code, not the person. "This assertion cannot fail" is welcome; "this is obvious" is not.
- Say when something is measured, projected or gated. Claiming a number you did not run is the one
  contribution offence this repository treats seriously, because its whole testing culture depends on
  trusting printed output.
- Assume the reader does not have your machine, your bandwidth, your dataset or your context.
- Accept that a Freedom Constraint may outlive your use case. If you disagree with one, argue it in a
  "Rejection of FC" section, as `CONTRIBUTING.md` describes.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal or political attacks on a contributor, publishing someone's private
information, and gatekeeping by tone. Also, in this project specifically: shipping a check that
cannot fail, and attributing work, data or a model to yourself that someone else produced.

## Scope

The conduct applies in issue and pull-request threads, in the commit history, in chat and at
events, and in any space where a contribution is being represented as this project's.

## Reporting

Report to the maintainers through a **private GitHub security advisory** (Security → Report a
vulnerability) — the channel stays private even if the subject is not a vulnerability, and a report
about a maintainer reaches the repository owner directly. Expect an acknowledgement within a week.

If a report concerns the only maintainer of the repository at the time, send it to any other
committer whose name appears in the file they touched, or open an issue that says only "conduct
report" and wait for contact; nothing of the report's content appears in the issue body.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this document, and may temporarily
or permanently bar a contributor from participating. Actions are explained to the person affected
before they take effect wherever that is safe to do, and the reason is recorded in the private thread
so a later contributor can see the standard that was applied rather than infer it.

Maintainers who do not follow this document in good faith may be removed from the project.
